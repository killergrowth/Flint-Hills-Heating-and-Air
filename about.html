Flint Hills Heating and Air Service

Local HVAC Company Serving the
Community Proudly

Flint Hills Heating and Air has provided exceptional heating and cooling services in El Dorado, Kansas and the surrounding areas for years. Our professional technicians are extremely skilled in all areas of the HVAC industry and provide outstanding service with exceptional results, excellent customer service, and competitive pricing.

Each member of our team takes great pride in keeping up to date on HVAC codes and the latest technology to help keep your home and family comfortable. We enjoy what we do and that is reflected in the quality of workmanship on every job, no matter the size or complexity. Our technicians handle everything from routine maintenance to full furnace and AC installations.

Why we standout from the rest

For all your heating and cooling needs, Flint Hills Heating and Air has been the first call in El Dorado since 2005. We also provide service throughout the surrounding areas. No job is too big or small and our technicians will be happy to help you find a solution.

  • Integrity and honesty from top to bottom.
  • Affordable and fair prices.
  • Licensed and Insured
  • We care customers and give personal attention
