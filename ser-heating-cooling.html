AC Repair & Installation

When your air conditioner stops keeping up with a Kansas summer, you need a technician who shows up fast and fixes it right the first time. Flint Hills Heating and Air repairs all major residential AC brands and systems, from minor refrigerant issues to full compressor failures, so your home stays comfortable no matter how high the temperature climbs.

If your system is beyond repair or simply too old to run efficiently, our team can help you select and install a properly sized replacement unit. We size every installation to your home's square footage and layout, not a one-size-fits-all guess, so you get the comfort and energy efficiency you're paying for.

We Give Best Solution

Our technicians diagnose the real problem before recommending a repair or replacement, so you never pay for work you don't need. Every job is backed by our workmanship guarantee.

  • Properly sized system installation
  • All major residential AC brands serviced

More Information

How often should I have my AC serviced?

We recommend a professional tune-up once a year, ideally in spring before peak cooling season. Regular maintenance catches small issues before they become expensive repairs and keeps your system running efficiently.

How do I know if I need a repair or a new system?

As a general rule, if your AC is over 10-12 years old and the repair cost is more than half the price of a new unit, replacement is usually the better long-term value. Our technicians will give you an honest comparison before you decide.

Why is my AC blowing warm air?

This is most often caused by low refrigerant, a dirty air filter, or a failing compressor. Our technicians can diagnose the exact cause on-site and give you a clear repair estimate before starting any work.

How long does a new AC installation take?

Most residential AC installations are completed in a single day. We'll walk you through sizing and system options up front so there are no surprises on install day.
