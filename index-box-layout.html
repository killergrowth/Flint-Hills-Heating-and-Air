Trusted local HVAC experts serving El Dorado and the surrounding area

Flint Hills Heating and Air has been keeping local homes and businesses comfortable since 2005. Our licensed technicians bring honest pricing, reliable workmanship, and fast response times to every heating and cooling job we take on.

From emergency furnace repairs in the dead of winter to AC tune-ups before the summer heat hits, our team treats every home like it's our own. We're proud to be the HVAC company El Dorado, Andover, Augusta, and Wichita homeowners trust.

Full-service heating and cooling solutions for every season

Beyond AC and furnace repair, we offer ductwork services, HVAC inspections, preventative maintenance plans, and diagnostics & troubleshooting to catch small issues before they become expensive breakdowns.

We also provide commercial HVAC services for local businesses, so whether it's a single-family home or a multi-unit property, we've got the right solution for your system.

Why homeowners choose Flint Hills Heating and Air

We're licensed, insured, and available 24/7 for emergencies. Our technicians show up on time, explain exactly what's wrong, and give you honest, upfront pricing before any work begins.

No surprise fees, no upselling you on work you don't need — just straightforward heating and cooling service backed by two decades of experience in the community.
