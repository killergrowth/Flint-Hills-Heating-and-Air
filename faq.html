Frequently Asked Questions

Discover you question from underneath or present your inquiry fromt the submit box.

How often should I schedule HVAC maintenance?

We recommend a tune-up twice a year — once for your AC in the spring and once for your furnace in the fall. Regular maintenance catches small problems early and keeps your system running efficiently.

Do you offer emergency HVAC service?

Yes, we're available 24/7 for emergency heating and cooling repairs. If your system fails outside of normal business hours, give us a call and we'll get a technician out to you as soon as possible.

What areas do you service?

We proudly serve El Dorado, Andover, Augusta, Wichita, and the surrounding communities. Give us a call to confirm we cover your specific location.

How do I know if I need a repair or a replacement?

If your system is under 10 years old and the repair cost is less than half the price of a new unit, repair is usually the smarter choice. Our technicians will always give you an honest recommendation based on your system's age and condition.

Why is my energy bill suddenly so high?

A spike in your energy bill often means your system is working harder than it should — usually due to a dirty filter, low refrigerant, leaky ductwork, or an aging unit losing efficiency. A diagnostic visit can pinpoint the cause.

How long does a typical repair take?

Most repairs are completed same-day. If a part needs to be special ordered, we'll let you know upfront and work to get it installed as quickly as possible.

Are your technicians licensed and insured?

Yes, all of our technicians are fully licensed and insured. You can count on trained, background-checked professionals for every job, big or small.

Do you offer financing options?

We do — give us a call to discuss financing options for larger repairs or full system replacements. We want to help you stay comfortable without breaking the bank.
